The Society for Cyberabad Security Council (SCSC) is a unique collaborative initiative formed by the Cyberabad Police Commissionerate and IT/ITeS companies to ensure the safety, security, and infrastructure resilience of the Cyberabad IT corridor. The Women Safety Wing is a core pillar focusing exclusively on women's well-being.

No. While She Shuttle routes focus heavily on major IT corridor corridors (Hitec City, Gachibowli, Financial District), services like Safe Stay guidelines and emergency response through Margadarshaks and SHE Teams are intended to benefit all working women, students, and citizens in the Cyberabad jurisdiction.

They create a connected safety loop: She Shuttle provides safe, monitored transport between your home/metro and workplace; Safe Stay ensures your PG or hostel is compliant with verified safety protocols; and Margadarshak provides a trained confidante in your office or community for guidance if you face harassment, POSH concerns, or safety worries.

For immediate life-threatening situations, always dial 112 immediately. For harassment incidents or stalker tracking, contact Cyberabad SHE Teams. For workplace and hostel concerns, you can connect with an SCSC Margadarshak or reach out to the SCSC Women Safety Wing helpdesk.
