About the Margadarshak Program

Building a compassionate, highly trained human bridge between citizens and law enforcement.

When women face distress—whether it is online harassment, stalking, workplace discrimination, or domestic challenges—fear of judgment, procedural intimidation, or lack of knowledge often delays timely help.

The Margadarshak ("Guiding Light") initiative transforms volunteers from corporate offices, academic institutions, and residential societies into certified safety guardians. Extensively trained by the Cyberabad Police, senior advocates, and mental health psychologists, Margadarshaks act as accessible, confidential first responders.

Who the Mentors Are: Margadarshaks are selected employees and leaders across SCSC member companies who undergo rigorous multi-day certifications covering POSH Act compliance, criminal law fundamentals, empathy counseling, and rapid liaison with Cyberabad SHE Teams.

Confidential assistance designed to empower you with clarity, protection, and legal rights.

100% Confidential First Contact

Engagement Process

How to Reach and Engage a Margadarshak

A safe, four-step path to obtaining clarity and resolving safety challenges.

  1. 1

    Find Your Sector Mentor

    Check your internal company HR intranet or consult the SCSC Margadarshak roster for mentors assigned to your business park. Margadarshak Directory Lookup: TBD

  2. 2

    Confidential Meeting

    Reach out via phone, secure email, or private meeting. Everything shared is treated with strict non-disclosure.

  3. 3

    Action & Option Planning

    The Margadarshak outlines your legal, organizational, and personal choices without forcing any action you are not comfortable with.

  4. 4

    Escalation & Support

    If you decide to file a complaint or request police protection, your Margadarshak accompanies and supports you throughout the process.
