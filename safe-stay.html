About Safe Stay

Transforming accommodation safety for thousands of women moving to Cyberabad for work and study.

With tens of thousands of young women relocating to Hyderabad annually to join multinational IT companies, pharmaceutical giants, and educational institutions, residential security is a critical priority. Unregistered and unverified hostels often suffer from absent security staff, broken surveillance, and lack of verified personnel.

Safe Stay is an institutional initiative spearheaded by the SCSC Women Safety Wing in coordination with local police stations. It creates a standardized, auditable framework that mandates strict safety guidelines, background checks of wardens and workers, and periodic on-ground audits.

Objective: To provide peace of mind to working women and their families by establishing transparent, certified, and police-inspected living standards.
Audit Checklist

Core Safety & Verification Criteria

Every participating hostel must adhere to rigorous structural and operational benchmarks.

Police Verification of Staff

Mandatory background screening and police verification for wardens, security guards, cleaning staff, cooks, and facility managers.

CCTV Surveillance Coverage

Round-the-clock camera monitoring at gates, reception, corridors, and perimeter fences with a minimum 30-day recorded footage retention.

Biometric / Regulated Access

Secure entry control via biometric scan or monitored visitor logs to prevent unauthorized male or unknown visitor ingress.

Fire & Structural Clearance

Compliance with local fire safety norms, functional extinguishers on every floor, clear emergency exits, and emergency lighting.

Verification Flow

How Safe Stay Operates

A collaborative audit cycle connecting hostel managements, residents, and enforcement authorities.

  1. 1

    Hostel Enrollment

    Hostel owners submit their licensing paperwork, staff rosters, and facility infrastructure details to the SCSC registry. Hostel Registration Form / Portal: TBD

  2. 2

    Joint Police Inspection

    A joint team of Cyberabad Police personnel and SCSC auditors conducts an unannounced physical verification of cameras, locks, and staff credentials.

  3. 3

    Certification & QR Tag

    Compliant accommodations receive the official Safe Stay certification certificate and an emergency-linked QR board placed at the entrance.

  4. 4

    Resident Voice & Audits

    Residents can report grievances or protocol violations anonymously, triggering spot checks and re-audit evaluations.

Got Questions?

Safe Stay FAQs

Answers for residents, tenants, and accommodation providers.

Look for the official SCSC Safe Stay QR certificate posted at the hostel reception or entrance. You can also verify hostel status directly with the SCSC Safe Stay Helpdesk. Online Hostel Directory URL: TBD

You can submit an anonymous report to the SCSC Women Safety Wing or notify the Cyberabad SHE Teams. SCSC coordinates with local precinct police to inspect the premises and mandate corrective actions.

Hostel operators can register through the SCSC Women Safety Wing desk, submitting staff documentation for police verification and scheduling a baseline premises inspection.

Safe Stay strictly focuses on physical security, police verification of staff, emergency preparedness, and tenant protection. Commercial pricing and meal contracts remain between the resident and the property owner.
