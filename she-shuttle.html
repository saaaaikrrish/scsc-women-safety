She Shuttle: Safe, Free Transit for Women in Cyberabad

A flagship mobility initiative by the Society for Cyberabad Security Council (SCSC) and Cyberabad Police, providing dedicated, technology-enabled, and secure transportation for female professionals and students.

Transit Security

About She Shuttle

Bridging last-mile transit vulnerabilities for working women commuting across the IT hubs of Hyderabad.

Launched by SCSC in active collaboration with the Cyberabad Metropolitan Police Commissionerate, She Shuttle was designed specifically to eliminate safety hurdles faced by female employees during non-standard shift hours and daily commute corridors.

Operating across key technology clusters—including HITEC City, Madhapur, Gachibowli, and the Financial District—each vehicle is monitored in real time and staffed with dedicated safety personnel. The service operates on a free-of-cost model for all women commuters.

Core Mission: To ensure that no woman in Cyberabad has to compromise her career or personal mobility due to lack of safe, reliable after-hours transportation.
Safety Standards

Key Safety Features

Engineered with layered physical and digital security mechanisms.

Real-Time GPS Tracking

Every shuttle is equipped with continuous GPS telemetry directly monitored by the Cyberabad Police Control Room and SCSC operational dispatch.

Female Security Marshals

Trained women security guards accompany passengers aboard the shuttles to ensure peace of mind and prompt handling of any onboard situation.

Surveillance & Panic Buttons

Equipped with interior and exterior CCTV cameras, as well as easily reachable emergency SOS panic alarms wired to rapid response.

Free of Cost for Women

Sponsored through member corporate contributions and SCSC safety funds—zero fare or ticket charges for female commuters.

How It Works

A simple, barrier-free boarding process for daily commuters.

  1. 1

    Locate Shuttle Stop

    Head to designated She Shuttle boarding points at metro stations, MMTS terminals, or recognized IT hubs.

  2. 2

    Track Real-Time Arrival

    Track approaching shuttles via the mobile tracker or look for the distinctive SCSC She Shuttle signage. App / Tracking Portal Link: TBD

  3. 3

    Board Safely

    Show your employee/student ID card to the female marshal onboard. Board freely with no tickets or charges.

  4. 4

    Reach Your Destination

    Alight comfortably at your office campus, hostel gateway, or transit hub under continuous CCTV and GPS escort.
